## The problem

A masthead sensor is a part that breaks — and the market that replaces it is closed.

## The approach

Wind is read in the way it disturbs an ultrasonic field between fixed transducers. The measurement is reciprocal: the medium cancels out, the wind remains. Nothing moves.

## Motion

A masthead moves — roll, pitch, acceleration. An on-board inertial unit corrects the measurement in real time: what reaches the helm is the wind, not the boat's motion.

## The horizon

An instrument open end to end — and, at the end of the road, the mixed-signal dies at its core rebuilt as free silicon. The measurement core returns to the commons as a reusable block.
